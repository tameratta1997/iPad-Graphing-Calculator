## 2. Description (Full - Max 4000 chars)
SaghaLive is your premium companion for tracking the precious metals market in Egypt. Stay ahead with real-time updates on Gold (24K, 21K, 18K) and Silver prices, calculated with the highest precision.

Key Features:
- **Live Market Prices**: Real-time tracking of Gold and Silver prices in EGP and USD.
- **Advanced Fee Calculator**: Calculate the total cost of bars and coins from major providers like BTC, SAM, and more, including making costs (Masnaia).
- **Market Analysis & Insights**: Get smart indicators and PNL (Daily Change) tracking to help you make informed investment decisions.
- **Smart Budget Planner**: Receive professional investment advice tailored to your wallet with automated allocation plans.
- **Provider-Specific Data**: Filter weights and fees for leading companies in the Egyptian market.
- **Multilingual Support**: Available in both Arabic and English.
- **Historical Charts**: Track price trends over various timeframes.

Whether you are an investor or just looking to buy a gold coin, SaghaLive provides the raw data and tools you need to calculate exact costs before you visit the jeweler.
